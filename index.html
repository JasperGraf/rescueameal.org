OUR MISSION

From food waste
to food support.

Every day, restaurants and food businesses may have safe, edible food left over at the end of service. At the same time, people in our communities struggle to get enough to eat.

Rescue A Meal aims to bridge that gap by helping rescue surplus food and connect it with people who can use it.

HOW IT WORKS

A simple idea with a big impact.

01

Restaurants donate

Food businesses let us know when they have safe, surplus food available.

02

We rescue it

Volunteers help collect and transport available food before it goes to waste.

03

People receive it

We work to get rescued meals to community organizations and people who need them.
